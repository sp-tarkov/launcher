Bundled here (self-hosted) so the launcher renders with the same typography on every platform
instead of relying on an OS fallback font. Files are the latin, latin-ext, and cyrillic subsets
of the weight-variable (wght 100-900) Roboto, sourced from @fontsource-variable/roboto.
